Executive Summary

Kabon.Africa is a digital carbon-credit marketplace and verification platform that connects African communities restoring degraded land and deploying clean energy with global carbon-credit buyers. We have designed, built, and deployed a complete, live platform — web and mobile apps, blockchain-based credit issuance, and satellite- and IoT-driven monitoring — and are now seeking [non-dilutive grant / pre-seed investment of US$XXX,000] to onboard our first cohort of verified projects and convert a working product into live, high-integrity carbon transactions.

The Problem

Africa holds an outsized share of the world’s nature-based carbon potential, yet captures only a small fraction of global carbon finance [insert current statistic + source]. The communities and local developers best placed to restore forests, savannas, grasslands and to deploy clean energy are largely locked out of the carbon market because:

  • Verification is costly and opaque — traditional MRV (measurement, reporting, verification) is slow and unaffordable at community scale.
  • Intermediaries capture the value — by the time credits reach buyers, little revenue returns to the people doing the restoration.
  • Buyers can’t trust quality — weak traceability fuels greenwashing concerns and depresses demand for African credits.

Our Solution

Kabon.Africa is a transparent, technology-driven marketplace that:

  • Onboards African land-restoration and clean-energy projects through a simple web and mobile workflow.
  • Verifies them with satellite vegetation analysis (NDVI via Copernicus), IoT sensor data, and a structured maker–checker verification process.
  • Issues tokenized carbon credits on-chain — traceable and tamper-evident — with formal retirement certificates.
  • Connects projects directly to global buyers, returning a far larger share of value to communities.

Why Now

  • Rapid growth in voluntary carbon markets and momentum behind the Africa Carbon Markets Initiative (ACMI).
  • Surging buyer demand for high-integrity, traceable, nature-based credits with credible MRV.
  • The convergence of mobile, satellite, and blockchain finally makes low-cost, trustworthy verification feasible at community scale.
